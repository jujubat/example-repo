- ✅ `RouteStopManager.reorder_stops()` - Reorder entire sequence
- ✅ `RouteStopManager.skip_stop()` - Skip stop for current journey (temporary)
- ✅ `RouteStopManager.reactivate_stop()` - Reactivate skipped stop

**Test Case**:
```python
# Add stop at sequence 3
stop = manager.add_stop(
    stop_name="New Station",
    location={'lat': -33.87, 'lon': 151.21},
    stop_type="station",
    sequence_number=3,
    estimated_stop_time_minutes=5
)
# Stops after position 3 automatically shift to 4, 5, 6...

# Remove stop
manager.remove_stop(stop_id="STP_003", reason="Closed for maintenance")
# Stops 4+ shift back to 3, 4, 5...
```
